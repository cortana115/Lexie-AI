Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install anything? ▼
No installation required! Lexie AI is available as a Microsoft Word and Outlook add-in. Simply visit the Microsoft AppSource store, search for Lexie AI, and add it to your Microsoft account. It will instantly appear in your Word and Outlook ribbons, ready to use.
Can I switch plans? ▼
Absolutely! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes take effect immediately, and we'll prorate charges based on your usage. If you upgrade, the additional features unlock instantly. If you downgrade, the change applies at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial for Professional? ▼
Yes! We offer a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan with full access to all features. No credit card required to start. After the trial ends, you can choose to continue with a paid subscription, downgrade to Starter, or cancel anytime.
What happens to my data if I downgrade? ▼
Your data is always secure. If you downgrade from Professional to Starter, your saved prompts, precedent bank, and memory features will be read-only until you upgrade again. Documents and basic data remain accessible. We never delete your information without explicit request.
Do you offer discounts for teams? ▼
Yes! We offer significant volume discounts for teams and law firms. Annual subscriptions save you 20% already, and teams of 5+ users can qualify for additional discounts. Contact our sales team for a custom quote tailored to your team size and needs.
